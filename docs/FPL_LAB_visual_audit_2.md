# FPL LAB: visual design audit 2 (follow-up to 17 Nov 2026)

**Date:** 18 Nov 2026 (GW11, commit `ee375fc` on `main`, after PRs #195 and #198–#206).
**Scope:** all 15 views at 1440 px and 390 px in dark mode, Home / Planner / Players at 1440 px and 390 px in light mode, and every changed screen (`week`, `planner`, `rivals`, `deadlines`, `review`, `fixtures`, `players`, `vsmodel`) at 1280 px in both themes. All 52 captures and 13 before/after pairs live in [`docs/audits/2026-11-18/`](audits/2026-11-18/). No application code, models or `history/` files were touched.

## 1. Summary

Of the 18 recommendations in the 17 Nov audit, **3 are fully fixed** (#2 Deadlines "Make these moves" wording, #7 Fixtures crest alignment, and #11 Players filter collapse), **2 are partly fixed** (#13 Home "Coin flip" card now explains what differs on Captain, and #17 Fixtures mobile controls wrap cleanly though the team column still takes 137 px of 320 px), **12 were not touched** in the #198–#206 redesign batch, and **1 regressed on phones** (#8 mobile header height grew from 242 px to 285 px because the new full-width deadline pill added a third row above the fold). On desktop, however, the #198–#206 redesign is a clear step forward: the pitch now sits high on Home beside a single "GW11 decisions" rail, Players collapses its filter block from ~250 px to 149 px (first row at y = 381 px) and adds a 5-GW xP heat strip, Planner puts the three strategy cards first and drops the duplicate Bank / Free transfers tiles, Rivals shows the points gap on phones, and past-GW pitch cards show full two-line names. Every automated gate passes (`npm test` 236/236, `npm run layout`, `npm run contrast` across 41,934 text elements, and `npm run taps` with 0 controls under 40×40 px on touch). The highest-priority items still open are the **hidden Status column on Health at 1440 px** (#1), the **285 px mobile header** (#8), the **clipped `GW10` label on the Model chart** (#3), the **1.44:1 light-mode owned-player yellow marker** (#5), and the **duplicate `@keyframes rise` / `pulse` definitions** (#4).

## 3. What the 18 Nov changes (#195, #198–#206) improved outside the 18 items

- **Home desktop layout (#198):** moving the 3 decisions into a right-hand rail (`GW11 decisions`) next to the pitch brings the pitch above the fold at 1440 px and 1280 px, and adds a read-only 5-GW plan preview strip (`GW11 49.4 xP` … `GW15 48.5 xP`) with strategy tabs (`Your plan`, `Balanced`, `Play safe`, `Chase rank`).
- **Players xP heat strip & active filter chips (#199):** the `xP next 5` column now includes a 5-square per-GW heat strip beside the total, and active filters appear as 40 px removable chips when the `Filters` drawer is closed.
- **Planner My team (#201):** `"What each strategy would do"` now sits at the top as three equal cards across at 1440 px / 1280 px, and the duplicate `Free transfers` and `Bank` boxes were removed.
- **Rivals mobile gap (#203):** below 768 px, the standings table shows each rival's points gap under their total (`Total / gap`), fixing a real phone usability gap without adding horizontal scroll.
- **Past-GW pitch names (#204):** Review and You vs the model now wrap long surnames (`Muharemović`, `Calvert-Lewin`, `Gibbs-White`) across two condensed lines instead of truncating to `"Muharem…"`.
